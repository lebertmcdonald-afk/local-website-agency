# Local Website Agency — Starter Kit

Your goal right now: **sign 1 paying client, by hand.** Not 5, not a robot. One.
Doing it manually first teaches you exactly what's worth automating later (in n8n).

This folder has everything you need to start today:

| File | What it's for |
|------|---------------|
| `lead-tracker.csv` | Open in Google Sheets. Fill it with businesses you find. |
| `qualification-checklist.md` | How to spot a perfect target in 10 seconds + how to score them. |
| `outreach-email-template.md` | The exact "I built you a sample" email + follow-up. |
| `call-script.md` | What to say when they reply or you call them. |

---

## Best trades to target (high reviews, weak/no website)

Pick **ONE** of these in **ONE** area you know. One trade only — your sample sites,
emails, and lingo all become reusable, and referrals compound inside a niche.

| Trade | Why it's a great target | Typical job value |
|-------|------------------------|-------------------|
| **Plumbers** | Word-of-mouth driven, tons of reviews, many have only a Facebook page | $150–800 |
| **HVAC (heating/cooling)** | High-ticket, always busy, sites are often years out of date | $300–6,000 |
| **Roofers** | Referral-based, project work, frequently no real website | $5,000–15,000 |
| **Electricians** | Often solo operators with great reviews and zero web presence | $150–2,000 |
| **Garage door repair** | Niche, high-urgency, high-ticket, weak web presence | $200–1,500 |
| **Landscaping / lawn care** | Many small operators living only on Instagram/Facebook | $100–5,000 |
| **Pest control** | Local, review-heavy, recurring-service businesses | $100–500 |
| **Tree service / arborists** | Project-based, referral-driven, often no site at all | $500–5,000 |
| **Appliance / mobile auto repair** | Strong reviews, ancient or missing websites | $100–1,500 |
| **Painters / pressure washing** | Portfolio lives on social media, no proper site | $300–5,000 |

**Easiest for a beginner:** plumbers, electricians, garage door repair, pest control —
lots of them per city, simple services, fast to build a sample for.

**Why these have no website:** the owner is in a truck doing the work all day. They've
been "meaning to fix it for years" and never had the time. That's your opening.

## Your first ~7 days

- **Day 1** — Pick 1 trade + 1 city. Find 20 businesses → fill `lead-tracker.csv`.
- **Day 2** — Score them with `qualification-checklist.md`. Star your top 3.
- **Days 3–4** — Build a 1-page sample site for each of the top 3 (v0.dev / Lovable, free).
- **Day 5** — Send the email from `outreach-email-template.md` (one at a time, personalized).
- **Days 6–7** — Reply, hop on a 10-min call (`call-script.md`), close. First client: $300–500.

When this starts working, come back and we'll automate the boring parts in n8n.

---

## Stay out of trouble (read once)

- **Send email slowly.** A handful a day from your normal Gmail is fine. Do NOT blast hundreds.
- **No AI robocalls.** Make follow-up calls yourself, by hand. (AI voice calls to strangers can be illegal in the US.)
- **Review management comes AFTER they sign.** You can only reply to a business's Google
  reviews once they make you a manager on their Google Business Profile.
- **Be honest in emails:** use your real name, and give them an easy way to say "no thanks."
