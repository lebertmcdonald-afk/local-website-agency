# Outreach Email Template

The magic: you're not *asking* if they want a website. You're *showing* them one you
already built. Personalize every email — never send the same generic blast.

**Rules:**
- Build the sample site FIRST, then email. The live preview link is the whole point.
- Send a handful a day from your normal Gmail. Don't blast hundreds (you'll get flagged).
- Use your real name and city. Give them an easy way to say "no thanks."

---

## Subject line (pick one — keep it lowercase + casual, looks personal)

- `built [Business] a website (free sample inside)`
- `[Business] — your [rating] stars deserve a real website`
- `made something for [Business]`
- `quick question about [Business]'s website`

---

## Email #1 (the first touch)

> **Subject:** built [Business Name] a website (free sample inside)
>
> Hi [First Name],
>
> I was looking at [trade]s in [City] and [Business Name] stood out — **[# of reviews]
> reviews at [rating] stars** is genuinely impressive. Clearly your customers love you.
>
> But I noticed you don't have a website yet (or the one you have looks a few years old).
> So instead of just telling you, I built you a quick sample to show what a modern one
> could look like:
>
> 👉 **[paste preview link here]**
>
> It uses your real services and a couple of your best reviews. If you like it, I can
> have it live on your own web address in about 10 minutes.
>
> Worth a quick look? Just reply and I'll walk you through it.
>
> [Your Name]
> [Your Phone] · [Your City]
>
> *(Not interested? Just reply "no thanks" and I won't follow up.)*

---

## Email #2 (follow-up — send 3 days later if no reply)

> **Subject:** re: [Business Name] website
>
> Hi [First Name] — quick bump in case this got buried. Here's that sample again:
>
> 👉 **[preview link]**
>
> Even if now isn't the right time, no pressure at all. Want me to keep it up for you,
> or take it down?
>
> [Your Name]

---

## Tips that make it work

- **Personalize the first line.** Mention something real — their rating, a specific
  service, or a recent review. It proves you actually looked.
- **One clear link, one clear ask.** Don't add five links or a price list. Just the
  preview + "want to see it live?"
- **Don't lead with price.** Get them to look at the sample first. Price comes on the call.
- **Stop after 2 emails.** If no reply, move on. There are millions of businesses.
