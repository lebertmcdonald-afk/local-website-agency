# 10-Minute Call Script — $500 Site + $500/mo Speed-to-Lead

Use this when calling a prospect (you call by hand — no AI robocaller, that can be illegal in the US).
You've done sales calls at ShipSlash — same muscle. Be a helpful neighbor, not a salesperson.
**The sample site already did the selling — you're just walking them through the door.**

> **Goal of the call:** book a 15-min Calendly slot OR get a verbal yes on the **$500** site.
> Calendly: https://calendly.com/lees-best10/15min

---

## 1. Opening (10 sec) — lead with THEIR proof
Pull the owner name + a real detail from their Google reviews (in the tracker) so it's warm:

> "Hey [Owner / there], it's Lee — I actually build websites for [trade]s here in [Area].
> I came across [Business] — **[rating]★ with [# reviews] reviews**, that's no joke — and noticed
> you don't really have a website out there yet. So I went ahead and **built you a free sample one.**
> Got 2 minutes? I'll text you the link right now."

→ Text the live link (`Site Link` in tracker) while they're on the phone. **Let them look.**

## 1b. No answer? Leave this voicemail (~20 sec), then text the link
Keep it short and warm — the goal is just to get them to look at the text you're about to send.

> "Hey [Owner / there], this is Lee — I build websites for [trade]s here in [Area]. I came across
> [Business] and your reviews, and I actually built you a **free website preview.** I'm gonna text
> you the link right now so you can take a peek. If you like it, call me back at **[your number]** or
> grab a quick 15-minute slot — the link's in the text. Thanks [Owner], talk soon."

**Right after the voicemail, send the SMS** (already in your tracker) so the link is waiting for them.
Mark the row `VM + text left` in the tracker and follow up in 2–3 days.

## 2. Discovery (let them talk)
- "How are most folks finding you right now — word of mouth, Google?"
- "Ever lose a job to the company that just shows up first online?"
- "Been meaning to get a real website sorted for a while?" *(almost always "yeah, for years")*

## 3. Present the value
> "Here's the thing — you've already got the hard part. **[# reviews] reviews at [rating]★** — people
> can't fake that. You just don't have a front door online for the person Googling '[trade] near me'
> at 9pm with a busted [pipe/roach problem/etc]. **That sample is your front door** — turns those
> searches into phone calls. And it's already built, with your real reviews on it."

## 4. Price — say it plainly, then STOP talking
> "To make it live and fully yours, it's a **one-time $500.** That's it — you own it."

Then **stay silent.** Let them respond first.

## 5. Plant the retainer seed (only after the site yes / if it fits)
> "Down the road — and no pressure today — the biggest thing I see hurt guys like you is **missed calls.**
> You're on a job, phone rings, you can't grab it, they call the next guy. I can set up a system that
> **instantly texts that caller back** so you never lose them. That's **$500/month** and it usually pays
> for itself in one saved job — but let's get your site live first."

*(That's the GoHighLevel "speed-to-lead" retainer — your recurring revenue. Tease now, close later.)*

## 6. Common objections
| They say… | You say… |
|-----------|----------|
| "How much again?" | "$500 one-time to make it live and yours. No subscription required." |
| "I'm too busy." | "That's the point — I already built it. I need 10 minutes and your OK, nothing else." |
| "I have a Facebook page." | "Great for regulars — but people searching Google can't book you there. This shows up the second they're ready to call." |
| "Let me think about it." | "Of course — the sample's live for you now. Want me to keep it up while you decide? I'll check back [day]." |
| "Can you do X too?" | "Yep — let's get the base site live first, then build from there." |
| "$500/mo is a lot." | "Totally optional, and only if missed calls are costing you. One extra job a month covers it. Site first." |

## 7. Close (ask for the yes)
> "Want me to make it live this week? Easiest is to grab 15 minutes here so I can get your details —"
> → send Calendly link. Or take the verbal yes + payment + go-live date right there.

If "not now" → "I'll leave the sample up, check back [day]."

## 8. Payment (when they say yes)
> "Perfect — I'll text you a secure payment link right now. It's **$250 to start, $250 when it goes
> live** — and the site is yours once it's up. Sound good?"

- **Only send the invoice AFTER a verbal yes on the call.** The invoice is the last step, never an opener.
  Square makes you confirm "the customer requested this by text" — that's only true once they've agreed,
  so texting an invoice to a cold prospect = false consent + a TCPA risk. Get the yes first, *then* send.
- Send a **Square invoice / payment link** by text or email — they pay by **card**. (Square is fastest;
  Stripe Payment Link or GoHighLevel invoicing also fine.) Testing the flow? Send to **your own number**, not a lead's.
- **Never take their card number over the phone** — always the link.
- **50% deposit ($250) up front, 50% ($250) on go-live.** (Full $500 up front is fine if they offer.)
- **Don't move the site to their domain / hand it over until paid** — the sample staying on your
  `nyc-home-pros` URL is your leverage.
- The invoice = your receipt + paper trail. Keep every one for your records.

## After the call — update the tracker
Set `Status`: `Booked`, `Won — $500 site`, `Interested`, or `Lost`. Note next step + date.
If Won: once live, ask for a quick testimonial — that's your proof for the next call. Then pitch the
$500/mo speed-to-lead once they've felt the site working.
