# Payment Setup — Collecting the $500 (Square)

How to invoice + get paid for the website. **Only ever send an invoice AFTER a verbal yes on the call.**

## One-time prep
- Square Dashboard → **Account & Settings → Business information**: set your **business name**
  (this is what shows on the customer's card statement + receipt — keep it recognizable).
- Connect your **bank account** (done ✅) — that's where payouts land.
- Bonus: Square's "fee-free on your first $1,000 / 180 days" promo unlocks once your first card payment clears.

## Create the invoice (recommended — handles the 50/50)
**Dashboard → Invoices & payments → Create invoice** (or Home → Quick actions → Send invoice):
1. **Customer:** add name/business + **mobile number** (no email = send by text) or email.
2. **Line item:** "Custom website — [Business]" · **$500.00** + short description
   ("Mobile-friendly site with your real Google reviews, built & hosted").
3. **Payment schedule → Request deposit** (use the **Amount $** tab):
   - **$250 — due Today** → name it "Deposit"
   - **$250 — Balance** (due "In 30 days" is fine; you'll collect it at go-live)
4. **Message:** "Deposit gets us started; balance when your site goes live."
5. **Save** → **Send** → choose **Text** or **Email**.

## Quick alternative — Payment Link
**Dashboard → Online → Payment Links → Create payment link → Accept a payment**:
- Title "Website deposit — [Business]", **$250** → Create → copy → text it. Make a second for the $250 balance.

## ⚖️ Ethical / correct rules
- **Verbal yes first.** Invoice is the last step, never an opener.
- Texting requires consent — Square's "customer requested this by text" box is only true after they've agreed.
  Sending an invoice to a cold prospect = false consent + TCPA risk.
- **Always send the link; never take a card number over the phone.**
- **Deliver before final payment** — go live, *then* collect the $250 balance. Never take money and stall.
- **Don't transfer the site to their domain until paid** — the sample on your `nyc-home-pros` URL is your leverage.
- **Set a refund rule up front:** "deposit is refundable until I start the build."
- **Keep every invoice/receipt** — it's your income record (Square may issue a 1099-K). NY web-design *services*
  generally aren't sales-taxed; don't add tax unless you confirm otherwise.
- **Testing the flow?** Send to your **own number**, not a lead's.
